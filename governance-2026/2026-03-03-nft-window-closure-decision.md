# Decision Record — NFT Window Closure
Date: 2026-03-03
Status: Final
Scope: Governance 2026

## Context
Legacy NFT metadata workflow accumulated structural complexity and mixed file states.
Operational clarity required a defined architectural boundary.

## Decision
The legacy NFT workflow is officially closed effective end of day 2026-03-03.

- No retroactive edits.
- No structural migration.
- Historical files remain unchanged.
- New standard (DGCP NFT Standard) begins 2026-03-04.

## Rationale
To reduce cognitive load, restore structural integrity, and establish forward-only governance discipline.

## Impact
- Legacy NFT artifacts remain valid but frozen.
- Future NFT artifacts must follow the DGCP NFT Standard structure.
- SHA-256 daily ledger serves as boundary anchor.

## Governance Principle
Forward-only.
Append-only.
No rewrite of historical truth.
