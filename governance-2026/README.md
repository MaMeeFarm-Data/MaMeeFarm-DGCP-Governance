## Governance Principles for 2026

Governance in 2026 adheres to the following principles:

- **Temporal clarity** — governance applies only within its stated period
- **Non-destructive continuity** — historical records are preserved
- **Append-only integrity** — changes are recorded, not overwritten
- **Human accountability** — governance remains human-authoritative
- **Audit readiness** — suitable for institutional and regulatory review
